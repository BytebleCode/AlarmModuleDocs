Alarm Module · Support

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Updated to cover attachments

Short version: Alarm Module doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, ads or trackers, and the app doesn't connect to the internet. Your alarms and calendar information stay on your iPhone.

What Alarm Module stores, and where

Your alarms, their titles, notes and attachments, your calendar connections and settings, and the list of past alarms in Recent are stored only on your iPhone. They're included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), which are handled by Apple under your Apple Account. The developer of Alarm Module never receives, sees or stores any of it.

Deleting the app permanently erases this data from your iPhone.

Calendars

If you allow calendar access, Alarm Module reads your events on your iPhone to show them, to ring alarms before the events you choose, and to keep alarms for connected calendars up to date. It copies only what an alarm needs (the event's title, time, location and notes) into its own storage on your iPhone. It never changes or deletes your calendar events, and never sends calendar data anywhere.

Photos and files

When you attach a photo or file, you pick it with Apple's photo picker or the Files app, and only the items you pick are copied into Alarm Module's storage on your iPhone. Alarm Module doesn't get access to the rest of your photo library. If you edit an attachment and save it, a copy is placed in the Files app in Alarm Module's folder on your iPhone. Copies inside the app are deleted after 60 newer alarms have rung. Attachments are never uploaded.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms are scheduled with iOS on your iPhone. The notification that comes with each alarm is created and scheduled on your iPhone; Alarm Module doesn't use push notification servers.

Background refresh

When iOS allows, Alarm Module runs briefly in the background to update calendar alarms. This happens entirely on your iPhone.

Children

Alarm Module isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
