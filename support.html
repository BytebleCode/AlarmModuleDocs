Alarms

My alarm didn't ring

Alarm Module's alarms are real iPhone alarms. They ring with a full-screen alert, in Silent mode and during Focus, even when the app is closed. If one didn't ring:

  • Open Settings → Apps → Alarm Module and make sure Alarms is allowed. If it's off, Alarm Module shows a banner in the Alarms tab with a button that opens Settings.
  • Check that the alarm's switch is on. An alarm that rings once switches itself off afterwards, like the Clock app.
  • Alarm volume follows Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone and Alerts.

Different times on different days

Make one alarm per time and pick its days under Repeat. For example, a 6:30 AM alarm repeating on Monday and an 8:00 AM alarm repeating on Saturday. You can have as many alarms on the same day as you like.

An alarm for a date far ahead

In the alarm editor, turn on Ring on a Specific Date and pick the day. It can be days, weeks, months or years away. The alarm rings once on that date at the time you set.

Titles, notes and the notification

Each alarm has a Title, shown on the ringing alarm, and Notes for extra details. When the alarm rings you also get a silent notification. Tap it to open the full title and notes in the app.

If the notification doesn't appear, allow notifications in Settings → Apps → Alarm Module → Notifications. iOS lets an app keep 64 notifications waiting at once, so if you have more alarms than that, the soonest ones get a notification first.

Snooze

With Snooze on, the ringing alarm has a Snooze button that rings again in 9 minutes. The countdown shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Calendar

An alarm for one event

In the Calendar tab, tap +, choose an event (use Show Events From to jump to any date), and pick when the alarm rings: at the start of the event or a set time before. You can add notes; they start as the event's own notes. If the event moves, the alarm moves with it.

Connecting a whole calendar

Tap Connect a Calendar and choose one or more calendars. Every future event in a connected calendar gets an alarm, including events you add later, until you disconnect the calendar.

  • Tap a connected calendar to choose how long before its events the alarms ring.
  • All-day events ring at 9:00 AM on the day. You can change the time, or turn off Include All-Day Events.
  • To change one event's alarm, tap it in the Calendar tab. To skip one event, swipe its alarm away.
  • Repeating events never end, so alarms are kept for events up to a year ahead, and that window moves forward every time the app syncs.

Calendar changes and syncing

When you add, move or delete events, Alarm Module updates their alarms when you open the app, while it's open, and in the background whenever iOS allows. The Calendar tab shows when it last synced.

  • Keep Settings → General → Background App Refresh on for Alarm Module so it can sync in the background.
  • If you've changed an event and want to be sure its alarm is up to date, open the app.
  • Alarms for events already set up keep ringing at their last known time even if the app hasn't run for a while.

Google, Outlook and other calendars

Alarm Module uses the calendars on your iPhone, so any account added in Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts (iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange and others) works. If the Calendar tab asks for access, allow Full Access, or change it later in Settings → Apps → Alarm Module → Calendars.

Recent

Finding a past alarm

The Recent tab lists every alarm that has rung, newest first, with its title and notes. Use the search field to search titles, notes, calendars and locations. The filter button changes the sort order (newest, oldest, or title) and filters by type (alarms, calendar events, or ones with notes) and by time. Swipe an entry to delete it, or use Clear History in the filter menu. Clearing history doesn't change your alarms.

Your data

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Your alarms, titles, notes and history are stored only on your iPhone and are included in your iPhone's own backups. Calendar events are read on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Deleting the app erases all of its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
