Alarm Module

iPhone alarms built around your schedule and your calendar.

  • Alarms that look and work like the Clock app's, and ring even in Silent mode and during Focus.
  • Pick the days and exact time for each alarm, set several alarms a day, or set a one-time alarm for any date, even years ahead.
  • Give each alarm a title and notes. When it rings, a notification brings up the details.
  • Ring an alarm before any calendar event, or connect a whole calendar so every event gets an alarm automatically.
  • See every past alarm in Recent, with search, sorting and filters.
  • Everything stays on your iPhone. No accounts, servers, ads or tracking.
